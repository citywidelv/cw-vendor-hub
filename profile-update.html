Update Your Company Profile

New phone, new email for your ledgers, new address, new bank, new office manager. Tell us here. A City Wide team member confirms every change with you at the contact we already have on file before anything is updated. Nothing changes automatically.

How this works

1. Pick your region and tell us who you are.  2. Check only the things that changed.  3. Send it. A City Wide team member reaches you at the phone or email we already have on file, confirms the change is really from your company, and then makes the update by hand. Bank changes are never applied without that call.

New Bank Account

Read this first

Do not type account or routing numbers anywhere on this page. Attach a bank letter on the bank's letterhead or a voided business check showing the account name, and the account name must match your company's legal name on your W-9.
